About

I am an M.S. student at the Data Science Institute, The University of Chicago, where I work with Prof. Andrey Kravtsov. I am also actively working with Dr. Chang-Goo Kim and Dr. Rajsekhar Mohapatra.

I received my B.S. in Physics from Zhejiang University in 2025, where I completed my thesis with Prof. Renyue Cen.

I use numerical simulations to study galaxy formation and large-scale structure. I am applying to Ph.D. programs in astrophysics for Fall 2027.

Research interests

I am broadly interested in theoretical and computational astrophysics, with a focus on galaxy formation and cosmology. Currently I am most interested in how small-scale physics, such as turbulence and stellar feedback in the interstellar medium, can inform the models used in larger-scale simulations.

Research

Turbulence in the multiphase ISM

2026–present

Princeton University, with Dr. Chang-Goo Kim and Dr. Rajsekhar Mohapatra

I decompose the turbulence in the TIGRESS simulations of the star-forming ISM into compressive and solenoidal modes with independent methods. I mask the hot gas before the decomposition to isolate the turbulent modes of the warm and cold phases, and relate them to feedback and star formation.

The baryonic mass dependence of thin disks

2025–present

The University of Chicago, with Prof. Andrey Kravtsov

I built a cleaning pipeline that deblends H i-selected dwarf galaxies in deep imaging. We measure their shapes and model how disk thickness depends on baryonic mass.

Planar structures in the ΛCDM universes

2023–2026

With Prof. Renyue Cen, in collaboration with Prof. P. J. E. Peebles

I developed a geometric probe for planar structures and we used Local Group analogs in N-body simulations to test the rarity of the observed local planar structures.

The quasar dipole in cosmological simulations

2026

I built mock quasar skies for Local Group analogs in N-body simulations and tested how unusual the observed quasar dipole and its misalignment with the CMB dipole are in ΛCDM.
